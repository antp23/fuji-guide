The XC lens has no aperture ring, so aperture is set with the front command dial. See the manual's "Choosing a Shooting Mode" for how Auto aperture is selected with this lens. Use exposure compensation for backlight, snow and bright skies.

Everyday setup

Files Suggested
JPEG Fine + RAW. JPEG carries the film sim; RAW is your backup negative.
Film sim dial Verified
PROVIA, Velvia, ASTIA, Classic Chrome, REALA ACE, ACROS, plus FS1–FS3 slots that can save recipes (20 sims total in menu).
White balance Suggested
Auto to start.
Auto ISO Suggested
Set a minimum shutter (1/250 moving kids, 1/125 people, 1/60 still scenes). It's a floor the camera may go below at max ISO, so watch shutter in dim light.
Autofocus Suggested
AF-S + single point for still subjects; AF-C + Face/Eye detection for moving people.
Burst Suggested
Low-speed (CL). Fastest rates use the electronic shutter and fill cards fast.

Shutter & stabilization

Default: mechanical shutter
Verified Mechanical to 1/4000; electronic to 1/32000 and silent.
Electronic shutter gotchas
Verified Fujifilm: may not suit fast subjects or handheld shooting; no flash. Can also band under LED light.
No IBIS
Verified No in-body stabilization. Stills rely on lens OIS; keep it on.
Video stabilization
Verified Digital stabilization, movie mode only; expect a crop.
Slow kit lens
f/6.3 at 33mm pushes ISO up indoors.

Simple video

Verified Up to 6.2K/30p, 4K/60p, Full HD/240p.
Suggested 4K 30p, AF-C with Face detection, OIS on, digital stabilization only when needed.

Before shooting: glance at the mode settings and the film sim dial. Odd results usually trace back to one of them.
